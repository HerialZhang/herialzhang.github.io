## About Me

I am an engineer and researcher interested in understanding AI.

My journey started from **mathematics and scientific instruments**, where I explored how artificial intelligence can discover hidden patterns from complex scientific data.

During my PhD at **Tsinghua University**, I worked on **AI for Science, mass spectrometry, and omics intelligence**, focusing on how artificial intelligence can accelerate scientific discovery.

Now, I am exploring the next frontier of intelligence — from algorithms and intelligent agents to **robots and embodied systems** that can perceive, learn, reason, and interact with the physical world.

I believe intelligence is not only about understanding data, but also about understanding and interacting with the world.
